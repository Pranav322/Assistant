# Analytics & Usage

Track how your chatbot is performing and how much it is being used.

## Usage Metrics

Navigate to the **"Analytics"** or **"Usage"** tab in your project to see:

-   **Total Requests**: The number of messages sent to your chatbot.
-   **Tokens Used**: The amount of "computational effort" used by the AI (both for reading your documents and generating answers).
    -   *Input Tokens*: Cost of reading the user's question and your knowledge base context.
    -   *Output Tokens*: Cost of generating the answer.

## Charts

-   **Requests over Time**: See peak usage hours or days.
-   **Token Usage**: Monitor your consumption against your plan limits.
